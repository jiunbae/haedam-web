Features

Automatic, but still your call

Automatic style picks

Haedam reads when a photo was taken and what's in it, and suggests a style for each one first.

Every number, in the open

Each style shows all its values, from exposure to HSL and curves. Nothing is hidden.

Make your own styles

Save the settings you like as your own style and share it with a link.

Batch edit

Pick several photos at once, and each one still gets its own adjustments.

Today's dump

Gathers the photos you took today into one set with a matching tone.

Privacy

All analysis and editing happen on your device. No photos are sent to a server.
