Privacy Policy

Your photos never leave your device

Effective October 15, 2026. This policy applies to the Haedam iPhone app and this website (haedam.jiun.dev).

Information we collect

Haedam has no sign-up, and it doesn't collect personal information such as your name, email address or device identifiers. I haven't added any ads, analytics tools or tracking either.

Photos and analysis

  • Photo analysis (brightness, color, scene recognition) and editing all happen on your iPhone. Your photos are not sent to a server.
  • The capture time and location (GPS) stored in a photo are used only on your device, to work out the sun's height and suggest a style.
  • When you create a photo to share, the setting that removes location and camera info is on by default.
  • My styles keep only small previews and the editing numbers, stored on your device.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photo library: used to make a dump from photos you took today, save your edited photos, and reopen the style of a photo you saved. It works even if you allow access to only some photos.
  • Camera: used only when you shoot in the camera tab with a live preview of the style.

Network use

  • The app downloads the community style list (feed.json) from this website. This request contains no photos or personal information.
  • Style links carry the editing numbers in the part of the address after the #. Browsers don't send this part to the server, so opening a link leaves no numbers on the server.
  • This website runs on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub may keep access logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Sharing with third parties

Since no personal information is collected, there is none to share with or sell to third parties.

Children

Haedam doesn't separately collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll post it on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions, please open a GitHub issue.
